Rooms

Add rooms and assign them as home rooms for specific classes (optional).

Rooms are Optional

Only add rooms if your institution has:

  • Shared facilities (labs, computer rooms, music rooms)
  • Rooms with limited capacity that need scheduling coordination
  • Classes that require specific room assignments

For regular classrooms assigned to specific classes, you don't need to add them here.

Step 7 of 7

Tips & Tricks

  • Give a name to the timetable.
  • Add your desired number of periods.
  • Optionally you can add the period timings also, not required.
  • Optionally you can add breaks too if you want them to be shown in the printed timetable.
  • Select the days when your institute is operational.
  • You can also switch to fortnightly timetable if your institute has bi-weekly schedule.
