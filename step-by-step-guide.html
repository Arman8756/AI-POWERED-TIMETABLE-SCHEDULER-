Step-by-Step Guide

Learn how to use the AI Powered Timetable Scheduler

Welcome to the Timetable Scheduler

Follow this 7-step guide to create your institution's perfect timetable in minutes. Each step builds on the last — just follow along and you'll have a complete schedule ready to go.

Step 1 — General Settings

Configure the basic parameters for your timetable

Choose Working Days

Select how many days per week your institution runs. You can pick individual days (Mon–Fri or Mon–Sat) by toggling the day cards on the settings tab.

Set Periods Per Day

Enter the number of teaching periods per day (e.g., 8). This determines how many lesson slots are available each day. The system supports 1–12 periods.

Move to Next Tab

Once settings look right, click the "Subjects" tab at the top or use the Next button to continue. You can always come back to adjust these later.

Tip: Most schools use 6 days × 8 periods = 48 total slots per teacher per week. The distribution charts on the view page will show how well each teacher's workload is spread across these 48 slots.
Step 1 of 7

Step 2 — Add Subjects

Define all the subjects / courses taught at your institution

Add Each Subject

Click "Add New Subject" and enter the subject name (e.g., Mathematics, English, Physics). Each subject gets a unique color that will appear in the timetable grid.

Edit or Remove

Use the edit (pencil) icon to rename a subject, or the trash icon to delete it. Subjects that are linked to lessons cannot be deleted until the lesson is removed first.

Bulk Import (Optional)

Click the "Bulk Import" button and paste a list of subject names — one per line. The system will create all of them at once, saving time for larger institutions.

You need at least 1 subject before you can generate a timetable. Add all subjects you plan to teach before moving to the Lessons step.
Step 2 of 7

Step 3 — Add Teachers

Enter all the teachers / instructors who will be assigned to lessons

Add Each Teacher

Click "Add New Teacher" and enter the teacher's full name (e.g., "Mr. John Smith"). Teachers are identified by their name in the timetable grid and substitute management system.

Bulk Import

Paste a list of teacher names (one per line) using the Bulk Import feature to add many teachers at once.

Teacher Substitute System

Every teacher added here will appear in the Teacher Substitute module. When a teacher is absent, you can assign any other teacher as a substitute for their scheduled periods.

Tip: You need at least 1 teacher. The more teachers you add, the more flexible the AI scheduler is when distributing lessons and suggesting substitutes.
Step 3 of 7

Step 4 — Add Classes

Define all student groups / grades that need a timetable

Add Each Class

Enter class names such as "Class 10A", "Grade 9B", or "Year 11 Science". The name you enter will appear as the row label in the class-wise timetable view.

Bulk Import

Add many classes at once by pasting names (one per line). Useful for institutions with 10+ classes.

Multi-Class Lessons

In the Lessons step you can assign a single lesson to multiple classes simultaneously — great for combined sections or assemblies.

Step 4 of 7

Step 5 — Add Rooms (Optional)

List classrooms or labs if you need room-level scheduling

Add Rooms

Enter room names or numbers (e.g., "Room 101", "Science Lab", "Computer Lab"). Rooms are optional but help avoid double-booking a physical space.

Bulk Import

Paste all room names at once for quick setup.

Rooms are optional — you can skip this step and still generate a complete timetable. If your school has dedicated labs or limited rooms, adding them helps avoid conflicts.
Step 5 of 7

Step 6 — Define Lessons

Link subjects, teachers and classes together with a weekly period count

Add a Lesson

Click "Add New Lesson". Select a subject, one or more teachers, one or more classes, and set the periods per week (e.g., 4 periods of Maths per week for Class 10A).

Multi-Teacher & Multi-Class

A single lesson can span multiple classes (combined sections) or be shared between teachers. The AI scheduler handles the assignment automatically.

Review Your Lessons

Switch between Class-Wise and Teacher-Wise views to review the lessons you've created and check for gaps before generating.

Bulk Import

You can also paste lesson data in bulk. Check test-bulk-import.txt in the project folder for the expected format.

Key rule: Every lesson needs at least one subject, one teacher, and one class. The total periods per week across all lessons should not exceed the total available slots (days × periods per day).
Step 6 of 7

Step 7 — Review & Generate

Check your data summary and let the AI build your timetable

Review the Summary

The Review tab shows a summary of all subjects, teachers, classes, rooms and lessons you've added. Check the counts match your expectations before generating.

Click "Generate Timetable"

Press the big Generate button. The AI scheduler allocates lessons to time slots while ensuring no teacher or class has double-bookings. A progress animation shows while it optimises.

View the Result

After generation you'll be taken to the View Timetable page. Switch between Teacher-Wise and Class-Wise views, check the distribution charts, and download the timetable as an HTML file.

Manage Substitutions

Back on the dashboard, click the orange Teacher Substitute card to record absences and assign substitute teachers for any scheduled period.

Tip: If some lessons remain unscheduled after generation, it means there were not enough free slots. Try reducing periods per lesson or removing lessons that overflow the available weekly slots.
Step 7 of 7
